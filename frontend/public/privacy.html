Your data supports your workspace. Growmiq mini processes customer conversations and business knowledge to provide AI assistance, inbox tools, and reporting. You control the accounts and information you connect.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Growmiq ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information in Growmiq mini at chat.growmiq.io, including account creation, support, and connected services. Growmiq mini is a Growmiq product.

For account administration and the operation of our service, we determine how information is used. When a business uses its workspace to handle its customers' information, that business generally determines the purpose of that processing and we process the information to deliver the service for it. The business's own privacy notices also apply.

If you are messaging a business that uses Growmiq mini, contact that business first about your conversation or personal information. You can also contact us using the details below.

2. Information we handle

  • Account and workspace information: names, email addresses, business details, user roles, settings, profile images, and authentication records.
  • Customer conversations: messages, customer names and contact details, channel identifiers, timestamps, delivery status, attachments, and conversation history received through channels you connect. Images, audio, or other files are processed where supported by the enabled features.
  • Business knowledge: documents, website content, instructions, FAQs, and other material you supply, together with search indexes and embeddings used to retrieve relevant information.
  • Workspace activity: agent settings, tickets, notes, feedback, assignments, conversation summaries, usage counters, and reports.
  • Integration credentials: access tokens, account and Page identifiers, API keys, and configuration needed to operate integrations you authorize.
  • Operational and payment information: IP addresses, device/browser information, security and error logs, support correspondence, subscription selections, transaction references, and payment status. Payment card details entered into a payment provider's checkout are handled by that provider.

Not every category is collected for every user. The information involved depends on your role, the features enabled, and the data you or your customers provide.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; deliver and route messages; retrieve business knowledge; generate AI responses and summaries; support human-agent handover; manage tickets and integrations; provide workspace analytics; administer subscriptions; investigate failures and abuse; and respond to requests.

We also use information to meet applicable legal obligations and protect the service and its users. Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the requested service, legitimate operational and security interests, legal obligations, or consent as appropriate. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully completed.

4. Connected platforms and permissions

When you enable an integration, you authorize access to the particular business account, Page, or channel and permissions selected during setup. We use that access to provide the features you request.

WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram: where connected, this can include account/Page identifiers, authorized profile information, messages, attachments, recipient details, and delivery events needed to receive conversations and send replies. Access remains subject to Meta's permissions, platform rules, and account availability.

TikTok: TikTok connection and video sharing are available only where enabled for authorized testing or approved use. Connecting requires your authorization. We process basic profile information and encrypted access/refresh tokens to maintain your connection. When you explicitly submit a video, we temporarily transfer its content, caption and chosen publishing settings to TikTok; we do not add that video to your business knowledge base. We retain publishing references for up to seven days to check delivery status. Public publishing and business inbox access depend on separate TikTok approvals. You can disconnect TikTok from Integrations.

Other integrations you choose may exchange information needed for their functions, such as support inbox messages, ticket details, store information, or CRM contacts. The integration's own privacy policy also applies.

You may disconnect supported channels in Settings → Integrations and revoke access in the connected platform's settings. Disconnecting stops future access through that connection; it does not automatically remove existing workspace records or content held by the platform. Use the deletion process below for stored Growmiq mini data.

5. AI processing and external models

When AI features are used, relevant prompts, messages, selected conversation history, business instructions, retrieved knowledge, and supported attachments may be sent to the AI provider configured for the workspace or supplied through the service. This processing is necessary to generate the requested response or analysis.

Depending on the configuration, providers can include Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or other supported model providers. Knowledge indexing may also use embedding models. An external provider's processing, retention, and model-improvement practices depend on that provider's terms and your account settings. Review those terms before choosing a provider or supplying confidential information.

Growmiq mini does not itself train a general-purpose AI model on your conversations. Workspace knowledge is indexed to support retrieval for the workspace. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate; a human should review consequential responses.

6. Who information is shared with

Information may be accessible to authorized workspace members and service operators who need it to provide support, maintain the service, or investigate security issues. Workspace administrators control team access.

We use service providers for hosting, storage, email delivery, optional push notifications, AI processing, and payment handling. Depending on enabled features, these can include our hosting provider, Google Firebase for notifications, configured AI providers, and PayHere for checkout. Connected platforms receive data needed for the actions you authorize. We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights and safety, or in a business transfer subject to appropriate privacy protections. We do not share connected-platform data for unrelated advertising.

7. Storage, international processing, and security

Information is stored in service databases, configured file storage, logs, and backups. Hosting and external service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

We use measures such as HTTPS, account authentication, workspace access controls, and encryption for stored integration secrets and conversation content. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your credentials, limit team access, and report suspected misuse promptly.

8. How long information is kept

We retain account and workspace information for as long as needed to operate the service, provide requested features, handle support, and meet legal or security requirements. Conversations and knowledge can remain in a workspace until removed through available controls or a deletion request. Disconnecting an integration or cancelling a subscription does not by itself erase all stored data.

When we carry out a deletion request, we remove or de-identify the relevant active records unless retention is legally required or necessary for a specific security, dispute, or transaction obligation. Residual backup copies are removed through backup rotation and are not used for ordinary service operations. Deletion from third-party platforms or providers is governed by their own controls and retention rules.

9. Your choices, rights, and data deletion

You can update available profile settings, manage workspace permissions, remove knowledge sources, and disconnect integrations using the relevant service controls. Workspace-wide deletion currently requires contacting support.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your information, restriction of processing, or object to certain uses. You may also raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To request account, workspace, or connected-platform data deletion:

Email deemal@checkfunnel.com with the subject Growmiq mini data deletion request. Include your account email, workspace name, the connected channel if relevant, and the information you want removed. Do not include passwords, API keys, or verification codes.

We may verify your identity and authority before acting, explain any retention exception, and confirm the outcome. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

If your information was provided through a business's workspace, ask that business to submit or authorize the request. To remove data held directly by Meta, TikTok, or another provider, use that provider's deletion controls as well.

10. Cookies, local storage, and notifications

The application uses cookies and browser storage where needed for sign-in, session continuity, security, interface preferences, and offline application resources. Optional push notifications use device/browser registration information after you enable them. You can revoke notification permission in your browser or device settings.

Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset preferences. These public policy pages do not load advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Separate marketing websites and connected providers may have their own cookie notices.

11. Children

Growmiq mini's business accounts are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect children's information to create business accounts. Businesses using the service are responsible for any age restrictions and notices applicable to their customers. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information inappropriately.

12. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as features, providers, or requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Where required, we will give notice of material changes and obtain any required consent before a new use of information.
